How a check runs

  1. Names are matched in Db2 by SQL; unknown names get a closest-match suggestion from a Db2 VECTOR_DISTANCE query.
  2. One SQL join finds every rule of each herb that applies to your medications' drug classes.
  3. LangChain retrieves supporting evidence through the Db2 vector store (DB2VS).
  4. A confidence gate sends weak evidence to review. Every check is logged in Db2 as counts and a digest, never raw names.
